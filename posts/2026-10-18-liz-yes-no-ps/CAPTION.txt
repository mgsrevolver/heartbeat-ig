Want more replies to your emails? End with a P.S. yes or no question. 🧡

Liz Wilcox shared this on a Heartbeat webinar: replies are like A-plus gold stars. Get your readers to reply and you land in the inbox again and again.

Her trick is a P.S. that takes one word to answer:
→ "Do you watch TMZ?"
→ "Do you currently own an RV?"
Hit reply, yes or no.

Keep it to a yes or no question, and keep it about opening a conversation, not closing a sale. 💛

Save this for your next email.
