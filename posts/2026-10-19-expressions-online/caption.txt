A paid community for people who photograph water. 🌊

EXPRESSIONS Online is built on Heartbeat by Steven Koppel, owner of EXPRESSIONS Gallery on Cape Cod. He has led photography workshops for nearly 10 years and has published three photography books.

→ Monthly live image reviews on Zoom, recorded for later
→ Private image critiques
→ Self-paced courses, plus Lightroom and Photoshop tutorials
→ Beginners welcome, and living near the ocean is not required

Whatever you teach, someone is looking for the room where they can learn it. Save this for later, and start yours at heartbeat.chat 🧡
