A community that stays within 50 miles of home. 🍎

Collectively Sustainable is for people building local food systems. Members pledge to buy from nearby growers, bakers and makers first, and to offer their own produce, products or services locally.

→ Courses on starting a local food initiative
→ A weekly Monday check-in
→ Monthly Each One Teach One sessions (October: apple cider vinegar)
→ A resource map app to barter, buy, sell or give away

A community can be as local as your neighbourhood. Save this for later, and start yours at heartbeat.chat 🧡
